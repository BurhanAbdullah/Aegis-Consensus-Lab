## Historical material
`docs/protocol_v4.md`, `docs/invariants.md`, and the archived `archive/final_run/**` artifacts are historical v4 material. They are retained for provenance and are not authoritative when terminology or equations differ from the tag4 canonical model.

In particular, the archived Recovery Elasticity Ratio claim is not treated as a theorem of the canonical model. The canonical risk equation uses explicit observable drift `D_{i,k}` and has an explicit containment condition.

## Publication rule
A manuscript statement is final only when it is supported by the canonical equation, executable implementation, and reproducible evidence. Unsupported physical-system claims, including the previously reported 9,450-case claim, are excluded until the raw case-level evidence and controller/network mapping are present.
