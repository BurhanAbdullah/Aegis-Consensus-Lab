## Where to obtain paper data

### Frozen round-level release traces

- `archive/final_run/experiments/results.csv`
- `archive/final_run/experiments/results_predictive.csv`

These are the frozen 200-round reference/predictive traces used by the publication-table generator. The generator validates schema, exactly 200 rounds, numeric fields, round IDs 1..200, and paired round identity before producing comparison artifacts.
